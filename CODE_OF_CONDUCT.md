# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the person you are replying to is trying to build something,
and argue with the work rather than with them.

## What is expected

- Take the strongest reading of what somebody wrote before answering it.
- Say what is wrong with a design, and say what you would do instead.
- Accept that a maintainer's "no" to a feature is about scope, not about you.
- Credit the work you build on.

## What is not

Harassment, personal attacks, sustained disruption of a thread, publishing
somebody's private information, and unwelcome attention of any kind. Deciding
that a rule was not meant to apply to you is also not.

## Enforcement

Maintainers will edit, hide, or delete comments and other contributions that
break this, and will say why. Repeated or severe behaviour ends in a ban from
the repository.

Report a problem privately to the maintainer, [@okian][owner], using the contact
details on that profile. Reports are handled discreetly, and the reporter is not
named to the person reported. Where the behaviour breaks GitHub's own terms,
[GitHub's abuse report form][abuse] is the faster route and can act across the
whole platform.

[owner]: https://github.com/okian
[abuse]: https://github.com/contact/report-abuse
